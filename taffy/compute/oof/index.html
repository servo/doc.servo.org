Shared positioning pass for out-of-flow (position: absolute / position: fixed) boxes.

Layout algorithms do not lay out their out-of-flow children directly (unless they are the child’s containing block). Instead they emit OofCandidate records which bubble up the tree via LayoutOutput::oof_candidates until they reach the box’s containing block, which lays the box out using the routine in this module.
