UnexpectedRestartOfDataChunkSequence

“11.2.4 IDAT Image data” section of the PNG spec says: There may be multiple IDAT chunks; if so, they shall appear consecutively with no other intervening chunks. UnexpectedRestartOfDataChunkSequence{kind: IDAT} indicates that there were “intervening chunks”.

The APNG spec doesn’t directly describe an error similar to CantInterleaveIdatChunks, but we require that a new sequence of consecutive fdAT chunks cannot appear unless we’ve seen an fcTL chunk.
