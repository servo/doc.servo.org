Expand description

Compute the root and remainder for n+1 limbs, given the root and remainder for n limbs (n=1 or 2). The input value must be normalized such that at least one of the top two bits is set, producing an s1 with at least Word::BITS/2 bits.

This is essentially a specialized version of the root expansion portion of sqrt_rem_normalized.
