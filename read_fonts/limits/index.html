Module limits

Expand description

Limits applied when interpreting font data.

Font files can describe structures that recurse, refer to themselves, or are simply enormous. Nothing here comes from the OpenType specification: these are the bounds this crate places on how much work one operation will do before giving up, so that a malformed or hostile font cannot turn a parse into a hang.
