Expand description

The design space of a multiple master font.

A multiple master font holds two to sixteen masters at the corners of a blend space with one to four axes. An instance is a weighted blend of the masters, and its weights come from a position in that space, given as blend coordinates from 0 to 1 on each axis or as design coordinates, which each axis maps to blend coordinates.

The conversions follow FreeType’s, including where its arithmetic departs from the specification, so that an instance draws as it does in FreeType.

pub fn design_to_blend(&self, design: &[Fixed], blend: &mut [Fixed])

Writes the blend coordinates for the design coordinates design to blend, one per axis.

Each design coordinate is rounded to a whole number first. An axis without a coordinate takes half its range above zero, (max - min) / 2, which is the middle only of a range that starts at zero. Both follow FreeType’s T1_Set_Var_Design, as does MasterAxis::design_to_blend.
