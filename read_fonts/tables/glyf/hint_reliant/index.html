Module hint_reliant 

Source
Expand description

Detection of fonts that need their TrueType instructions to draw correct contours.

These are often called “tricky” fonts. They are recognized by family name or by the checksums of the cvt, fpgm and prep tables. The lists of names and checksums come from FreeType.
