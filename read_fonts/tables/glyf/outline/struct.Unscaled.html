Struct Unscaled 

Source 
pub struct Unscaled;
Expand description

Leaves coordinates in font units.

Variations still apply, and composite components are still transformed and placed, but nothing is scaled or rounded to a pixel grid. This is what an autohinter wants: the outline as the designer drew it, at the requested location in variation space.

The arithmetic matches Scale26Dot6 at no size, without the round trip through 26.6 that getting font units back out would otherwise need.
