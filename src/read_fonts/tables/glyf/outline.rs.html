1//! Building outlines from `glyf` data.
2//!
3//! There are two entry points:
4//!
5//! - [`Outline`] owns its buffers and grows them as needed. A caller supplies
6//!   a glyph and a size.
7//! - [`OutlinePlan`] allocates nothing. It walks the composite tree, reports
8//!   how much space the result needs through [`buffer_lengths`], and
9//!   [`load`s][load] into buffers the caller supplies. Use it to control where
10//!   the memory comes from, or to supply a [`Hinter`].
11//!
12//! Both read through an [`OutlineContext`], which states the tables and the
13//! variation location. [`OutlineTables`] implements it by reading a font.
14//!
15//! A load produces an [`OutlineRef`], which borrows the caller's buffers.
16//!
17//! [`buffer_lengths`]: OutlinePlan::buffer_lengths
18//! [load]: OutlinePlan::load
