https://storage.spec.whatwg.org/#storage-quota The storage quota of a storage shelf is an implementation-defined conservative estimate of the total amount of byttes it can hold. We use 10 GiB per shelf, matching Firefox’s documented limit (https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Storage_API/Storage_quotas_and_eviction_criteria).
