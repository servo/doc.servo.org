When encoding strings, we use variable-size encoding per the following table

Chars 0 - 7E are encoded as 0xxxxxxx with 1 added Chars 7F - (3FFF+7F) are encoded as 10xxxxxx xxxxxxxx with 7F subtracted Chars (3FFF+80) - FFFF are encoded as 11xxxxxx xxxxxxxx xx000000
