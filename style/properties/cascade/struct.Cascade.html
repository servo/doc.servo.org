Resolves the custom properties and applies the prioritary properties in a single cycle-tracked walk: as a custom property depending on em/lh/the used color-scheme becomes resolvable, substitute_all applies the prioritary property it needs (via ensure_prioritary_property) so it computes against the right value. Any prioritary property not triggered that way is applied at the end.
