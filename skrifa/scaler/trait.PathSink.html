Expand description

Receives the path commands for an outline.

To receive the outline’s points in font units, use UnscaledOutlineSink.

fn quad_to(&mut self, cx0: f32, cy0: f32, x: f32, y: f32)

fn curve_to(&mut self, cx0: f32, cy0: f32, cx1: f32, cy1: f32, x: f32, y: f32)

Emit a cubic bezier segment from the current point with control points at (cx0, cy0) and (cx1, cy1) and ending at (x, y).
