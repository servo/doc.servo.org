§htmlelement: HTMLElement§rel_list: MutNullableDom<DOMTokenList>

The relations as specified by the “rel” attribute

§relations: Cell<LinkRelations>

The link relations as they are used in practice.

The reason this is separate from HTMLLinkElement::rel_list is that a literal list is a bit unwieldy and that there are corner cases to consider (Like rev="made" implying an author relationship that is not represented in rel_list)
